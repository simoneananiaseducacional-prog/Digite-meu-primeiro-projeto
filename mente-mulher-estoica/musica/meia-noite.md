# Meia-Noite (rascunho)

Simone Ananias feat. Dario Kael. Pop sombrio com violão dedilhado em Lá menor (~100 BPM).
Tema pedido por ela: nada sobre ela nem sobre a vida dela. A música fala da noite e da
cidade que acorda à meia-noite; ninguém é o "eu" da história.

## Estilo

```
Dark acoustic pop anthem in A minor, 100 BPM. Starts with haunting fingerpicked acoustic guitar alone, mysterious and cinematic, chord progression Am - Dm - C - F. Slowly builds with deep sub-bass, soft cinematic drums, finger snaps and dark atmospheric pads, then grows into a powerful catchy chorus while the fingerpicked guitar keeps playing. Chanted "Meia-noite" hook, nightlife mood, magnetic and fun, never sad. Male and female duet, voices low, dark and firm.
Sung in Brazilian Portuguese with neutral international pronunciation. Keep the lyrics exactly as written.
Exclude: sertanejo, pagode, funk, gospel, MPB, happy pop, ukulele, sweet voice, melisma, sad ballad.
```
