# Poucos, Mas Certos (rascunho)

Terceira música da Simone. Mesma linha da Cidadela e da Fortaleza: dance pop escuro, grave forte, dueto.
Créditos: **Simone Ananias feat. Dario Kael**.
Tema: ser verdadeira afasta muita gente, mas deixa por perto as pessoas certas.
Gancho para grudar na cabeça: "Poucos, mas certos".

**Ao gerar no Suno:** música NOVA (não Cover), voz salva "Voz de Simone Ananias",
Influência de estilo e do áudio em ~70%. Cole o ESTILO e a LETRA sem mudar nada.

## Estilo

```
Male vocal: monstrous giant voice, extremely deep bass, guttural, gravelly, growling power, menacing and huge, half-spoken, never soft.
Female lead vocal: commanding mature woman, low alto, deep velvety smoky tone, firm, authoritative, raspy grit, never sweet.
Dark electronic dance-pop power anthem, 124 BPM, deep heavy sub-bass, punchy four-on-the-floor kick, minimal dark synths, cinematic drums, sparse arrangement. Slow dark intro that builds for 20 seconds before the beat drops. Big chanted "Poucos, mas certos" hook, catchy and repetitive. Strong and confident, never sad.
Sung in Brazilian Portuguese with neutral international pronunciation. Duet. Keep the lyrics exactly as written.
Exclude: soft male voice, tenor, boyish voice, thin voice, sweet or girly voice, melisma, ballad, rock, distorted guitars, sertanejo, gospel, pagode, MPB.
```
