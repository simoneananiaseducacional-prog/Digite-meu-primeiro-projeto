# Enigma (rascunho)

Simone Ananias feat. Dario Kael. Pop sombrio com violão dedilhado em Lá menor (~100 BPM),
referência: vídeo do violonista @dragunovilya. Tema leve e magnético, sem sofrência e sem
história pessoal: uma mulher que ninguém consegue decifrar.

## Estilo

```
Dark acoustic pop anthem in A minor, 100 BPM. Starts with haunting fingerpicked acoustic guitar alone, mysterious and cinematic, chord progression Am - Dm - C - F. Slowly builds with deep sub-bass, soft cinematic drums and dark atmospheric pads, then grows into a powerful dark pop chorus while the fingerpicked guitar keeps playing. Catchy chanted "E-nig-ma" hook. Sensual, magnetic and confident, never sad. Male and female duet, voices low, dark and firm.
Sung in Brazilian Portuguese with neutral international pronunciation. Keep the lyrics exactly as written.
Exclude: sertanejo, pagode, funk, gospel, MPB, happy pop, ukulele, sweet voice, melisma, sad ballad.
```
